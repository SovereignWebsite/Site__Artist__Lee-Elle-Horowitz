Your website

A draft, 25 November 2026

2 / 13

Where it comes from

Built from your own words

  • Every word comes from the text you supplied, and a few facts from your Spotlight profile.
  • Nothing is invented: no prices, no pupils, no reviews, no qualifications you did not give.
  • No contact details are shown until you choose them.
  • Every page says Draft until you approve it.

Design 1

Alef-bet

The home page for now. A large Hebrew letter opens each section, the whole alphabet runs across the page, and your nine kinds of lesson are letter tiles.

Design 2

Conversation

The page as a chat: the questions a parent asks, and your answers, one by one.

Three tenses

Past, present and future, the way you teach grammar: your story, lessons today, and where a student wants to get to.

7 / 13

Design 4

The stage

From your acting training: a theatre programme, with Hebrew as the main act and music in the second half.

8 / 13

Design 5

Notebook

A ruled exercise book: one plain column, for a parent who wants the facts, and it prints cleanly.

9 / 13

Two languages

Every design, in English and in Hebrew

The English / עברית switch sits at the top of every page, with a button that stays in the corner. The Hebrew pages read right to left. The Hebrew is a translation of your text, marked as not yet checked by you.

10 / 13

On any screen

Made for phones first

Most parents arrive on a phone. Every page fits a phone screen, works in light and dark mode, and passes the accessibility checks.

11 / 13

Your decisions

What we need from you

  • Which design you like best, or which parts of which.
  • Corrections to the Hebrew, and your name in Hebrew if you want it.
  • Your own photos, with the photographer's name for each.
  • Which contact details, if any, visitors should see; prices and availability only if you want them shown.
  • Your approval, when you are happy.

12 / 13

Next

How to send changes

  • For now, send them to Dinis.
  • A form on the site, for changes, photos and visitors' enquiries, is on its way.
  • Later, your own Claude can make changes for you, following the rules this site keeps.

13 / 13

It is yours

Plain files, in a place you own

No server, no cookies, no tracking. The files, the history and the address can move anywhere, at any time.
