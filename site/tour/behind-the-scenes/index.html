  2. factory.bcreates the repository and the address
  3. The keeperb.site.lee-elle-horowitz builds and keeps the site
  4. design.bwrites the design brief
  5. design.b.roundsreviews each round with synthetic parents
  6. hub.blists the site once she approves it

The pipeline

From request to live site

  1. Requestfiled in the Sovereign Website factory
  2. Repositoryseeded with checks and a deploy workflow
  3. Own keythe keeper signs every commit with its own key
  4. Buildpages generated from JSON
  5. Checksrun before every push
  6. Livea push to dev publishes in a minute or two

4 / 8

Content

Every word lives in JSON

  • site/data/content/en.json: her words, as she supplied them.
  • site/data/content/he.json: the Hebrew translation, for her to check.
  • site.json, start.json, tour.json and admin.json: everything else.
  • tools/designs/build.py turns them into plain HTML pages, in both languages. It holds no words of its own.

en.json + he.json → build.py → 10 design pages, 2 home pages, 2 pickers

5 / 8

Quality

Checked before every push

  • Build, link previews and internal links.
  • HTML validity, and accessibility (axe) on every page, in light and in dark mode.
  • No em dashes, no family strip, a leak scan for secrets.
  • No page longer than 8,000 px on a phone, and no sideways scrolling.
  • Nothing loaded from another website: no fonts, scripts or trackers.

6 / 8

Design

Five ideas, then review rounds

  • design.b's brief asked for five designs, each from its own idea, not colour skins of one layout.
  • design.b.rounds asked synthetic parents: would you book a Hebrew lesson for your child here?
  • Round 1 put Hebrew letters in every design, the facts parents look for higher up, and a light version of The stage.
  • Round 2 fixed the alphabet direction, removed empty placeholders and gave the site one voice: hers.

7 / 8

Trust

Signed, and public

  • Each agent makes its own signing key; only the public half is published.
  • Every commit is signed and carries the agent's name and session.
  • The keeper's notes, open work and decisions are in the repository, for anyone to read.

8 / 8

Sovereign

A website its owner controls

Static files in one repository: no server code, no cookies, no tracking. It belongs to Lee-Elle, and can move anywhere.
