Your words

Your words live in one place, so a change is made once

  • Your words are kept, as you gave them, in one English file
  • The Hebrew translation sits in a matching file, for you to check
  • Every page, in every design and both languages, is made from those two files
  • So a word is changed once, by you or your AI, and every design follows

CConductor Synthetic presenter, made by code, no real person

Every word of Lee-Elle's is kept in one place, one file per language. The pages are made from it, so nothing is typed twice.

Two languages

Your Hebrew pages read right to left, properly

Not a mirror image: each Hebrew page is marked as Hebrew and right to left, and the layout follows.

  • One setting turns any of the five designs right to left
  • English names keep their own direction inside Hebrew
  • Checked by a synthetic Hebrew-speaking persona, and by Lee-Elle once she reads it

Nothing invented

What we left out, on purpose

  • No prices, pupils or reviews: she has not given us any yet
  • No phone number or email until she chooses which to publish
  • Every fact comes from her own profile and her school's page; every photo is credited to its source
  • The Hebrew is marked on every page as not yet checked by her
