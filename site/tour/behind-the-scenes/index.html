5 / 11

Content, in JSON

Your words live in one place, so a change is made once

  • site/data/content/en.json: her words, as she supplied them.
  • site/data/content/he.json: the Hebrew translation, for her to check.
  • site.json, start.json, tour.json and admin.json: everything else.
  • tools/designs/build.py turns them into plain HTML pages, in both languages. It holds no words of its own.

en.json + he.json → build.py → 10 design pages, 2 home pages, 2 pickers, the start page, the tour (3 pages) and the admin page

CConductor Synthetic presenter, made by code, no real person

Every word of Lee-Elle's is in a content file, one per language. The pages are built from those files, so the HTML holds none of her words.

Two languages

Your Hebrew pages read right to left, properly

Not a mirror image: each Hebrew page is marked as Hebrew and right to left, and the layout follows.

  • One stylesheet turns any of the five designs right to left
  • English names keep their own direction inside Hebrew
  • Checked by a synthetic Hebrew-speaking persona, and by Lee-Elle once she reads it

Nothing invented

What we left out, on purpose

  • No prices, pupils or reviews: she has not given us any yet
  • No phone number or email until she chooses which to publish
  • Every fact comes from her own profile and her school's page; every photo is credited to its source
  • The Hebrew is marked on every page as not yet checked by her

CConductor Synthetic presenter, made by code, no real person

What we leave out matters as much as what we put in. Everything here can be traced to where it came from.
