The team

One request, a team of agents, each with one job

Each agent does one job and keeps its notes in public, so its thinking stays sharp and anyone can see who did what.

  1. routessites.bTakes the request, checks it is complete, starts a dedicated keeper. Never builds.
  2. createsfactory.bMakes the repository, the address and its certificate.
  3. briefsdesign.bWrites the design brief: five ideas, not colour skins.
  4. buildsThe keeperb.site.lee-elle-horowitz builds and keeps the site, in its own session.
  5. testsdesign.b.roundsReviews each round with synthetic personas, including an Israeli-born parent reading the Hebrew.
  6. listshub.bLists the site in the hub once she approves it.

Design

Five ideas, then rounds with synthetic parents

design.b.rounds puts each version in front of synthetic personas and asks one question: would you book a Hebrew lesson for your child here?

  1. BriefFive designs, each from its own idea
  2. Round 1Hebrew letters in every design; the facts parents look for higher up
  3. Round 2The alphabet right to left; no empty placeholders; one voice, hers
  4. Wow passEach design opens differently, with her photo on the first screen
  5. Round 3Conversation and Notebook rise to 3 of 5; phone fixes

5 / 9

Content

Every word lives in JSON

  • site/data/content/en.json: her words, as she supplied them.
  • site/data/content/he.json: the Hebrew translation, for her to check.
  • site.json, start.json, tour.json and admin.json: everything else.
  • tools/designs/build.py turns them into plain HTML pages, in both languages. It holds no words of its own.

en.json + he.json → build.py → 10 design pages, 2 home pages, 2 pickers, the tour and the admin area

Two languages

Right to left, properly

The Hebrew pages are not a mirror image of the English ones: the html element is marked lang="he" dir="rtl", the CSS uses start and end instead of left and right, English names inside Hebrew sit in their own direction, and Hebrew type is set heavier and larger.

  • One stylesheet per design, plus one that turns any of them right to left
  • A switch at the top of every page, and a button that appears as you scroll
  • Checked by a Hebrew-speaking persona in every round

7 / 9

Quality

Checked before every push

  • Build, link previews and internal links.
  • HTML validity, and accessibility (axe) on every page, at desktop and phone width, in light and in dark mode.
  • No em dashes, a leak scan for secrets.
  • No page longer than 8,000 px on a phone, and no sideways scrolling.
  • Nothing loaded from another website: no fonts, scripts or trackers.

Trust
