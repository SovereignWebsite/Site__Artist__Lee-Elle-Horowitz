South African-born and Israel-raised, I am a fluent Hebrew speaker who grew up in Israel and later moved to the UK. I have experience teaching Hebrew to children and beginners, both online and in person.

I offer personalised Hebrew lessons that focus on building confidence, developing conversational skills and understanding how the language works in everyday life.

My approach is creative, interactive and student-centred. Rather than simply memorising vocabulary, I help students understand how to construct sentences, communicate naturally and develop a practical understanding of Hebrew.

Teaching approach

Lessons are tailored to each student's age, ability and goals. I aim to make learning enjoyable, practical and engaging, using conversation, activities and real-life examples. I want students to feel comfortable making mistakes, asking questions and gradually developing confidence using Hebrew independently.

Lesson format

  • Online lessons via Zoom
  • Individual tuition
  • Small group lessons, subject to arrangement
