Your website, and what happens next

This is a first draft of a website for your Hebrew tuition. Every word on it comes from the text you supplied, and nothing is final until you say so. Here is how to shape it.

1

Choose a design

The same words, in six different pages. Open each one on your phone, then say which you like best, or which parts of which.

  1. א1. Alef-betThe page as an alphabet chart: a large Hebrew letter opens each section.
  2. …2. ConversationThe page as a conversation: questions a parent asks, and her answers.
  3. →3. Three tensesPast, present and future, the way she teaches grammar.
  4. ★4. The stageA theatre programme, from her acting training: Hebrew first, music in the second half.
  5. ✎5. NotebookA ruled exercise book: one column, plain and printable.
  6. ע6. Ivrit (Hebrew)The whole page in Hebrew, right to left. Translated for her to check.

2

Check the words, and the Hebrew

Read your text on the page and tell us anything to change. Your own words are kept as you wrote them.

The pages use only single Hebrew letters for now. A wrong Hebrew word on a Hebrew tutor's site would cost trust, so no Hebrew words go up until you have checked them. If you would like some, for example your name in Hebrew or a phrase for the Conversation design, send them and they will be added exactly as you write them.

3

Send photos, text and changes

For now, send them to Dinis, who set this site up. A form on this site for sending changes and photos is on its way.

  • Photos. The photos on the drafts are stand-ins, to show what the pages can look like. Send your own, and for each one say who took it, so it can be credited.
  • Contact details. None are shown. Say which, if any, you want visitors to see. Until then, the button on each page says Enquiries open soon.
  • Prices. None are shown, and none will be unless you give them.

4

Approve it

When you are happy, say so. The Draft notice comes off and the site is yours to share.

5

Who looks after the site

A dedicated site agent, b.site.lee-elle-horowitz, builds and keeps this site, working with the Sovereign Website design agents. Its notes on what it has done and what is open are public: the keeper's notes.

Later: changing it yourself

You never have to, but you can. The whole site is a set of plain files in one place on GitHub that you will own: github.com/SovereignWebsite/Site__Artist__Lee-Elle-Horowitz. No server, no cookies, no tracking, and you can move it anywhere.

Connect GitHub
  1. Make a GitHub account at github.com/signup, if you do not have one.
  2. Send your GitHub login to Dinis. You get two invitations: one to your site, with permission to change it, and one to join the SovereignWebsite organisation as a member. Membership gives you no access to anything else; it lets your own Claude work with your site.
  3. Accept the first: signed in to GitHub, open your site's page on GitHub and press View invitation on the banner, or use the link in the email.
  4. Accept the second at github.com/orgs/SovereignWebsite/invitation ("Join SovereignWebsite"). Use this address: the link in the email may not offer the button.
  5. If you use Claude, open its GitHub settings and press Check again next to SovereignWebsite.
Make a change on GitHub
  1. Open the site's folder and pick the page (the home page is index.html).
  2. Press the pencil, change the words, then press Commit changes.
  3. The site updates by itself in a minute or two.
Let your own Claude make changes

Give it this prompt. It tells your Claude the rules this site keeps. See also Use with your AI.

Ask for another pass

Tell Dinis, or open an issue saying what you would like changed. The site's agents work from that record.
